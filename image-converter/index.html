Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I convert JPG images to WebP online?

+

To convert JPG to WebP, drag and drop your JPG files into ToolMatic's Image Converter, click the 'WebP' format pill, and download your converted files individually or as a complete ZIP archive. The conversion completes in milliseconds directly in your browser.

What happens to transparent backgrounds when converting PNG to JPG?

+

Because the JPEG standard does not support alpha transparency channels, any transparent pixels in your source PNG image will be automatically filled with a solid white background during conversion.

Can I convert multiple images at the same time?

+

Yes. ToolMatic supports batch conversion for up to 50 images simultaneously. All selected files are converted in parallel and can be downloaded together in a single ZIP file.

Are my files uploaded to a remote server during conversion?

+

No. All format decoding and encoding execute 100% locally on your computer or phone using the browser's Canvas API. No image data is ever uploaded to any external server.

Which format should I convert my website images to?

+

Convert your website images to WebP. WebP offers 25% to 35% smaller file sizes than standard JPEG at identical perceived quality, supports transparent backgrounds, and is natively supported by over 98% of web browsers.
