How to Batch Compress Multiple Images at Once for Free

Compressing images one by one is exhausting. Learn how to process entire folders of photos in seconds using 100% private client-side batching.

In Short

To compress dozens of images at once, drag and drop all your files together into ToolMatic's Image Compressor. Our client-side queue processes each photo asynchronously across your CPU cores and lets you download the entire optimized collection in a single ZIP file.

Batch Workflow Benefits

E-commerce sellers, photographers, and web designers regularly manage hundreds of photos. Traditional cloud uploaders make you wait for slow network uploads for every single file. ToolMatic's client-side batch processor processes files right on your machine with zero server bottlenecks.

Frequently Asked Questions
