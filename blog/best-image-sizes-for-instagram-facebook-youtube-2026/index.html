Best Image Sizes for Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube in 2026

Never post pixelated or awkwardly cropped graphics again. Here is your definitive 2026 social media dimensions cheatsheet.

In Short

In 2026, the ideal social media resolutions are: Instagram Square Post (1080 × 1080 px), Instagram Portrait (1080 × 1350 px), Stories & Reels (1080 × 1920 px), Facebook Feed Post (1200 × 630 px), and YouTube Video Thumbnail (1280 × 720 px under 2 MB).

2026 Social Media Dimensions Reference Table

Platform Asset Type Dimensions (Pixels) Aspect Ratio
Instagram Square Feed Post 1080 × 1080 px 1:1
Instagram Portrait Feed Post 1080 × 1350 px 4:5 (Most Screen Real Estate)
Instagram / TikTok Stories & Reels 1080 × 1920 px 9:16
Facebook Landscape Feed Post 1200 × 630 px 1.91:1
YouTube Video Thumbnail 1280 × 720 px 16:9 (Max 2 MB)

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is the 1080x1350 portrait format recommended for Instagram?

+

The 4:5 portrait aspect ratio (1080 × 1350 px) occupies the maximum vertical screen space on mobile phones as users scroll their feeds, generating higher engagement than square photos.
