How to Convert JPG to WebP Online in Seconds

Upgrade your images to Google's preferred format. Learn how to batch convert JPG files to WebP client-side without quality loss.

In Short

To convert JPG to WebP online for free, drag and drop your photos into ToolMatic's Image Converter, select 'WebP' in the format dropdown, set the quality slider to 80%, and download your compressed files individually or as a complete ZIP archive. Everything happens client-side in your browser.

Why WebP is Superior to JPEG

JPEG was standardized more than thirty years ago in 1992. WebP was designed specifically for the modern internet. It uses predictive encoding to analyze adjacent pixel blocks, enabling 25% to 35% smaller file sizes at identical perceptual sharpness.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will converting JPG to WebP make my website faster?

+

Yes. Because WebP files are on average 30% lighter than equivalent JPEGs, your web pages transfer fewer megabytes over cellular networks, leading directly to faster load times.
