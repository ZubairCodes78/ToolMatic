Why Large Images Are Killing Your Website Speed and Google Rankings

Heavy images are the number one cause of sluggish web performance. Discover how bloated graphics drain your revenue and how to resolve them effortlessly.

In Short

Large image files saturate mobile network bandwidth, delay browser main-thread decoding, and severely inflate Largest Contentful Paint (LCP) times. Every 1-second delay in mobile page load reduces conversion rates by up to 20% and lowers your Google search rank.

The Cost of Megabyte Bloat

Modern broadband hides website slowness on desktop computers, but over 65% of global internet traffic browses on mobile phones with fluctuating 4G connections. An unoptimized 6 MB header photo forces mobile phones to spend seconds downloading and decoding millions of unnecessary pixels.
