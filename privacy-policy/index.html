Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026. Last Updated: October 2026.

Privacy Summary

ToolMatic does not store, collect, or transmit your uploaded images or graphics to any remote server. Everything is processed 100% locally in your web browser. This policy also outlines how third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

1. Zero Server Upload Architecture

Unlike conventional web services, ToolMatic is engineered entirely around client-side browser processing. When you compress an image or convert a graphic format on toolmatic.site:

  • Your files are read locally into your web browser's memory using JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API.
  • No file data is uploaded to, transmitted across, or stored on our servers.
  • Once you close or refresh your browser tab, all temporary file memory is immediately cleared by your operating system.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookie Disclosure

ToolMatic is a 100% free service funded through display advertisements served by Google AdSense and other trusted advertising partners. To ensure compliance with Google's publisher policies:

  • Third-party vendor cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to ToolMatic or other websites on the internet.
  • DoubleClick Cookie: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting Out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting AboutAds.info or Your Online Choices.

3. Web Analytics

We use Google Analytics (gtag.js) to understand general website traffic trends, such as page views, country-level geography, browser types, and referral sources. This telemetry is aggregated, anonymized, and does not contain personal identifiable information (PII) or details about the files you process.

4. Log Files

Like standard web servers worldwide, our hosting infrastructure automatically logs basic technical request data when a web page is requested, including IP addresses, browser user-agents, referring URLs, and timestamps. These logs are used solely for server health monitoring, security defense, and DDoS mitigation.

5. GDPR and CCPA / CPRA Compliance

Under the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA):

  • Because ToolMatic does not collect, sell, or rent your personal data or user files, your privacy rights are safeguarded by design.
  • You have the right to request information about cookie usage, opt out of non-essential advertising cookies, and request that no personalized tracking occur.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect technical or legal modifications. The latest revision date will always be prominently noted at the top of this document.

7. Contact Information

For questions or requests concerning our privacy practices, please contact us at support@toolmatic.site.
