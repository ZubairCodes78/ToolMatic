About ToolMatic

Fast, free, and privacy-focused image compression and conversion tools for creators, photographers, and developers.

In Short

ToolMatic is a 100% free web utility platform focused on client-side image compression and format conversion. Because all processing executes locally in your browser, your files and photos are never uploaded to remote servers.

Our Mission: Fast Image Processing with Complete Privacy

At ToolMatic (toolmatic.site), we believe basic digital utilities should be instantaneous, completely free, and genuinely private. Most traditional online conversion and compression services force users to upload their personal photos and graphic files to remote cloud servers. This introduces unnecessary security risks, file retention worries, and slow upload queues.

ToolMatic takes a fundamentally different engineering approach: client-side browser computing. By leveraging modern Web APIs — including the HTML5 Canvas API, Web Workers, WebP/AVIF hardware acceleration, and WebAssembly — our tools execute directly on your machine's CPU and GPU. Your files never touch a remote server, cannot be intercepted in transit, and are never saved on our disks.

Core Principles of ToolMatic
  • 100% Free Forever: No subscriptions, no hidden paywalls, and no feature gating.
  • Strict Client-Side Privacy: Your images stay strictly on your device.
  • No Forced Sign-Ups: No registration, passwords, or personal email addresses required.
  • Clean & Transparent: No deceptive buttons, popunders, or shady redirects. We are funded purely through transparent, standard display advertising.

The Two Core Tools We Build

ToolMatic focuses purely on doing image tasks with exceptional speed and privacy:

  • Image Compressor: Compress JPG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF photos up to 90% without visible loss in quality, with target size modes and social presets.
  • Image Converter: Convert between JPG, PNG, WebP, and AVIF formats in batch mode with customizable lossy quality.

Technology Behind Our Platform

ToolMatic is built purely with standards-compliant HTML5, modular CSS, and lightweight modern JavaScript. We integrate trusted, audited open-source browser libraries via public CDNs, such as JSZip for client-side archive packaging.

We welcome feedback, suggestions, and feature requests. Feel free to contact us anytime at support@toolmatic.site.
